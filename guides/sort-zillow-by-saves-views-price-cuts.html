Can you sort Zillow by saves, views or price cuts?

Zillow has no sort for saves, views or price cuts. How to compare those signals by hand, and how Caza adds a sort menu for them to search results.

How-to guide5 min readUpdated November 2026

Short answer: No. Zillow’s own sort menu orders results by price, newest, bedrooms, bathrooms, size and lot, plus its own Homes for You order. There is no sort by saves, views or price cuts. To compare those signals you open each listing, or use an extension. Caza adds a sort menu with Demand Score, Most Saves, Most Views, Most Days on Market and Most Price Drops.

What can you sort by on Zillow?

Zillow’s sort menu on search results is built around the listing’s basic facts: Homes for You, Price (High to Low), Price (Low to High), Newest, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Square Feet and Lot Size. That is the full list we saw on a homes-for-sale search on zillow.com in November 2026. Zillow can change it, so open the Sort menu on your own search to check.

What you won’t find there is a way to rank homes by how many people saved them, how many viewed them, or how often the price has dropped. Those numbers appear on the listing page, one home at a time.

How do you compare saves, views and price cuts without a tool?

You can do it by hand. It is slow, but it works for a short list.

  1. Sort the search by something close to what you want, such as newest, and shortlist 10 to 15 homes.
  2. Open each listing and note the days on Zillow, the views, the saves and any price changes in the price history.
  3. Put the numbers in a sheet with one row per home. Add a column for saves per day (saves divided by days listed).
  4. Sort your sheet. Read the top rows as homes to check first, not as predictions.

Some listings hide parts of this. If saves or views are not showing, see how to see how many people saved a Zillow listing for where they appear and what to do when they don’t.

Why do saves, views and price cuts matter when you compare homes?

Each one is a reason to look closer, not a forecast of what will happen.

  • Saves and views show how much attention a home is getting. A home with a lot of saves for its age may draw competing offers, so you may want to move sooner.
  • Days on market shows how long it has been listed. A long stay can mean the price or condition is putting buyers off, or just a slow season. See what days on market means on Zillow.
  • Price cuts show the seller has already moved. A cut is a prompt to read the price history and ask why. See what a price cut tells you.

None of these tells you the condition of the home or what the seller will accept. Use them to decide which listings to read first.

Which sort should you start with?

It depends on what you are trying to learn. If you are a buyer who worries about competition, start with Most Saves or Demand Score to see which homes other buyers are watching. If you hope to negotiate, try Most Days on Market and Most Price Drops to find sellers who have already moved or waited a while. If you are an agent checking a client’s area, Most Views shows where attention is going. Change the sort and compare: a home that appears near the top of several lists is the one to open first.

What are the limits of sorting by these signals?

Saves and views depend on what Zillow shows for that listing, and a new listing has had little time to collect any. A high count is a reason to check, not proof that a home is a good buy. Compare homes of the same type in the same area, and confirm the price against nearby sales before you act.

FAQ

Can you sort Zillow by saves?

Not with Zillow's own sort menu (checked November 2026). Saves and views show on each listing page, so you compare them one listing at a time or use an extension.

Can you sort Zillow by price cuts?

Zillow's sort menu has no price cut option (checked November 2026). You can read the price history on each listing, or use a sort that counts drops, such as Caza's Most Price Drops.

Is there a way to sort Zillow by days on market?

Zillow shows days on market on each listing. Caza adds a Most Days on Market option to the sort menu on Zillow search results.
