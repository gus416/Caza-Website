What Caza adds to a listing

Caza is a free Chrome extension for Zillow. Open any listing and it adds four things on the page you're already looking at: a Demand Score, sales comps, rental comps and investor numbers. No tabs to switch, nothing to export.

Demand Score

See which listings buyers are circling

A 0–100 Demand Score on every listing in your search, built from saves, page views and days on market, with a small bonus when saves and views are coming in fast. Hover a score for the breakdown, then sort the whole page by it.

  • Price cuts and back-on-market listings flagged
  • Sort by score, saves, views, days on market or price drops
  • Listing agent and phone in the hover card

Sales comps and the report

Comps without leaving the listing, and a report with your name on it.

The drawer

Click Sales comps and Caza reads Zillow's own Recently Sold search around the home. Your listing stays pinned as row one, the closest matches are starred, and every sale says how it compares.

  • Condos, houses, 2–4 units and 5+ units never mixed
  • Widen to a mile or add older sales in one click
  • Add a sale Caza left out, or drop one that doesn't fit

The report

Print a one- or two-page PDF from any comps drawer: the median sold price and $/sqft, where your listing sits among the sales, a distance sketch, and every comp with the reason it was picked. Your name, brokerage and phone go in the header.

Rental comps print the same way, and Copy summary gives you plain text for an email or a text.

Not an appraisal. Sale prices can be missing in some states, and Caza says so. Check your MLS before client use.

Rental comps

Asking rents, with every comp shown

From a for-sale listing or a rental, Caza pulls similar active rentals nearby, matched on bedrooms and property type, and shows the typical range. These are asking rents for active listings, not signed leases.

  • Send the median, or one comp, to the investor numbers
  • Compare my rent, in the toolbar popup, for your own unit
  • California listings note where rent caps may apply

Investor numbers

The math, with every input labeled

Cap rate and monthly cash flow on homes; price per unit, GRM and cap rate on multifamily. Taxes and insurance start from state estimates you can edit, and every figure says where it came from.

  • Debt coverage and cash-on-cash return
  • Your down payment, rate and term, saved as defaults
  • Interest-only loans; multifamily income never uses the Rent Zestimate

For renters

Know the real monthly number before you apply.

On a rental listing, Caza adds the all-in monthly cost with the required fees, then shows how the rent compares with similar places nearby, using asking rents for active listings.

  • All-in cost: rent plus required monthly fees, and the effective rent after any free weeks
  • Higher or lower than similar rentals nearby, with the median and each comp listed
  • A rent far below the area gets a reminder to verify the listing before paying anything

Where the numbers come from

Caza reads the Zillow page you're on and Zillow's own Recently Sold and rentals searches. It never guesses quietly: every input in the investor numbers carries one of five tags.

Private by design

The extension has no account, no Caza servers and no tracking. Its settings stay in your browser, and it only loads pages from zillow.com. This website uses Google Analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

What is Caza?

Caza is a free Chrome extension for Zillow. On a listing it adds a 0–100 Demand Score built from saves, views and days on market, nearby sales comps, rental comps from active asking rents, and investor numbers such as cap rate, cash flow and DSCR. It also prints a PDF comps report. It runs in your browser with no account and no servers.

Which listings does Caza work on?

Zillow listings in the US: houses, condos, townhomes, 2–4 unit and 5+ unit buildings, manufactured homes, land, and rental listings, including apartment buildings.

Does Caza work on Redfin or Realtor.com?

No. Caza works on zillow.com only, in Chrome on desktop.

What does it cost?

Caza is free right now.

How is the Demand Score calculated?

From the listing's own saves, page views and days on market, with a small bonus when saves and views are arriving quickly. Hover any score to see the breakdown. It measures buyer attention on Zillow, not the home's condition or price.

Is the comps report official?

Not an appraisal, and not a CMA. It shows sales as Zillow shows them, with the filters used and anything set aside. Treat it as a starting point and check your MLS before client use.

What about states where sale prices aren't public?

In Texas and other non-disclosure states, Zillow often has no sale price. Caza says so plainly instead of filling the gap, and Texas reports include the state's required notice.

Does Caza touch my Zillow account?

No. It reads public listing pages in your browser and never signs in or changes anything on Zillow.

Are the rents real leases?

No. They're asking rents for active listings on Zillow, and Caza labels them that way everywhere they appear.

Can I use my own loan terms and costs?

Yes. Change the down payment, rate, term and costs on any listing, and save your taxes and insurance as the default for that state.

Is Caza made by Zillow?

No. Caza is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Zillow Group.
