How to join

Membership starts from the Caza popup in Chrome: click Join and choose monthly or yearly. The website can’t start checkout, because ExtensionPay ties the membership to your Caza install. Add Caza to Chrome first if you haven’t.

What counts as a report

Sales comps and rental comps are counted separately, 3 of each a month. One report is one listing a month (or, for an apartment building or an address you type, one address): the first time Caza shows comps for it. Changing the distance, adding older sales, reopening, printing or copying that listing never uses another. A search that fails (Zillow’s check, an expired click, no results) never counts. Both counts start over on the 1st.

Restore after a reinstall or on a new computer

Members: Caza popup → “Already paid? Log in”, with the email you paid with.

Cancel anytime

Caza popup → Manage membership. Your membership stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are in the terms of sale.

Seller and payment

Caza, not Google, sells the membership. Payment is handled by ExtensionPay on Stripe; Caza never sees your card. Sales tax may be added where Caza is required to collect it. See the privacy policy for what ExtensionPay and Stripe receive.

Questions

Is there a trial?

No, there is no trial. Without a membership you get 3 sales comps and 3 rental comps reports every month, plus the Demand Score, sorting, Investor numbers and the renter view.

Do I need an account?

No Caza account. Members check out with an email on ExtensionPay, and use the same email to log in on another computer.

What happens if I cancel?

Reports stay unlimited until the end of the period you paid for. After that you are back to 3 sales comps and 3 rental comps reports a month.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You get a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first payment, monthly or yearly. After that there are no partial refunds unless the law requires one, and billing errors are always refunded. See the terms of sale.
