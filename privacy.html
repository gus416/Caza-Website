Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: October 28, 2026 (version 2.9.0)

Summary. The Caza Chrome extension runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no advertising and no Caza servers, and it never collects or uploads listing data in the background. What you set and type stays in your browser, with one small exception: Chrome Sync stores a single marker (see below). The extension loads pages from Zillow, described below. Five things can reach anyone else, and each is stated plainly: (1) Google Maps links you click, (2) the map page you open with Show all properties on map, (3) the Chrome Sync marker, (4) the Caza welcome and uninstall pages on this website, and (5) usage counts, which are off unless you turn them on. This website, usecaza.com, is separate and uses Google Analytics; see Website analytics below.

This page mostly describes the Caza Chrome extension. The website, usecaza.com (including the welcome and uninstall pages), uses analytics, described under Website analytics below.

What Caza reads

Caza reads listing data already loaded on the Zillow pages you visit (saves, views, days on market, price, size, property type, location, listing description, and similar fields) to calculate scores, comps and investor numbers. Listing descriptions are read on your device to find unit counts, rents and condition words, and on rental listings to find stated concessions (for example "1 month free") and lease length; the text itself is not stored, and it is not sent anywhere.

On rental listings and Zillow's rentals search, Caza reads the asking rent, bedrooms, bathrooms, size, days listed, rent changes, required monthly fees and apartment-building floor-plan prices that the page shows. Rental comps use active for-rent listings only, never past leases. Caza does not read, show or filter anything about schools, crime, demographics, source of income or familial status.

Network requests

Caza's own requests go to Zillow. Caza does not collect or upload listing data in the background. The exceptions that go elsewhere are listed after the Zillow requests.

Requests to Zillow

  • Scores on results pages. To read saves and views, Caza loads the Zillow page of listings that are on screen or about one screen away, a few at a time and at least 300 ms apart. Listings read in the last 24 hours are not loaded again. If Zillow answers with an access-denied or rate-limit response, Caza stops these requests for 10 minutes. Caza never interacts with a "confirm you're a person" check.
  • Sales comps. Only when you click Sales comps (on a listing, or View Sales Comps in a score tooltip), Caza opens Zillow's own Recently Sold search page in a new tab, the same page you could open yourself, and reads the sold homes that page shows. Widen the distance and Older sales are another click that loads a wider Recently Sold search page.
  • Rental comps. Only when you click Rental comps (on a listing, a rental listing or an apartment building page), or click Find asking rents in the popup's Compare my rent, Caza opens Zillow's own rentals search page around that place in a new tab, the same page you could open yourself, and reads the active rentals that page shows. Widening the search is another click that loads the wider rentals search page.
  • Get details for these comps (for multifamily also Get unit mix for these comps). Only when you click it and confirm, Caza loads the Zillow listing pages of the comps shown, plus any sales left out only because their estimated unit count differs, at most 12, one at a time about 1.5 seconds apart, and stops at once if Zillow blocks a request. What it reads (unit count, unit mix, condition and sale-type words) is kept with the 24-hour listing cache below.

Comps and PDF reports also show listing photos. Your browser loads them directly from Zillow's image servers (zillowstatic.com), using the photo links Zillow already shows on the listing; Caza adds nothing to those requests. Nothing is sent to Caza.

Exceptions, each started by you or optional

  • Google Maps links. When you click a Google Maps link in Caza, your browser opens a Google Maps search for that property's address. Google then receives the address and handles it under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you click.
  • Show all properties on map. When you click it, Caza opens usecaza.com/map in a new tab with the comps in the part of the link after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to our server. Map tiles load from tiles.openfreemap.org, which sees your IP address and the map area you view. The map page has no analytics.
  • Chrome Sync marker. Caza stores one small marker, caza_gf, in Chrome Sync so that existing users keep their free status on their other devices. It holds no listing or personal information. Chrome Sync is run by Google under your Chrome settings.
  • Welcome and uninstall pages. When Caza is installed it opens usecaza.com/start once (nothing opens on updates), and when it is uninstalled Chrome opens usecaza.com/bye. The link carries only Caza's version number, never listings, IDs or anything about your use. These are pages on this website, covered by Website analytics below. The uninstall survey uses Netlify Forms.
  • Usage counts. Off unless you turn them on. See Opt-in usage counts below.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep the settings above in your browser, and the one Chrome Sync marker.
  • Access to https://www.zillow.com/*: to show Caza on Zillow pages and make the zillow.com requests described above.

Caza does not use the tabs permission and cannot read any other website. Its background service worker records the install and plan status above when Caza is installed or updated, opens the welcome and uninstall pages described above, lets Caza use session-only storage when the browser starts, and sends the optional usage counts only if you turned them on. It does not request listing data.

Opt-in usage counts (version 2.9 and later)

Usage counts are off unless you turn them on, in the Caza popup or on a one-time card shown on Zillow. When on, Caza sends feature counts (which features were used) with a random ID to Google Analytics. It does not send addresses, prices, listing IDs or page links, and the ID is not your name. Turn them off at any time and the random ID is deleted from your browser.

If you uninstall, Caza opens a short, voluntary feedback page on this website. It asks for no personal data: one multiple-choice reason, an optional free-text box and the Caza version number. Whatever you type in the box is stored as written with Netlify Forms, so please leave personal details out.

Third parties

For the extension: Zillow (the pages you visit and the comps searches you click), Google (Maps links you click, Chrome Sync, and the opt-in usage counts, which are off by default), OpenFreeMap (map tiles on the map page you open) and Netlify (the uninstall survey, if you submit it). The extension has no advertising and no data sale. The "Rate Caza" button opens the Chrome Web Store review page only when you click it. Links in the rental comps drawer (the FTC's advice on rental listings, and official rent-cap sources for California, Santa Ana and Los Angeles) open those sites only when you click them.

Limited Use. Caza's use of information it reads from Zillow pages or that you type adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Caza uses this information only to provide its single purpose: showing buyer-demand scores, comps and investor numbers on Zillow. It does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, personalized or re-targeted ads, creditworthiness or lending, and does not transfer it to anyone except as described on this page: the Google Maps links and map page you open yourself, the Chrome Sync marker, and the optional usage counts. No person at Caza reads your listing data, because Caza does not collect it.

Website analytics (usecaza.com)

This section is about this website, including the welcome page (usecaza.com/start) and uninstall page (usecaza.com/bye) that the extension opens. The extension itself sends analytics only if you turn on the opt-in usage counts described above.

The comps map page (usecaza.com/map) has no analytics. The comp list travels in the part of the link after the # sign, which browsers don't send to our server; the map images come from OpenFreeMap.

usecaza.com uses Google Analytics to count visits, pages viewed, where visitors came from, and clicks and interactions such as store links, buttons, menus, videos, FAQ answers and calculator use. With each of these, Google receives the page path (never the part of the address after a question mark), your browser and device type, the site that linked you here, and your IP address, which Google uses for an approximate location and does not store. Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off, and we don’t use the data for advertising.

The calculators run in your browser. The numbers you type into them are not sent to us or to Google; analytics only records that a calculator was used. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_*) in your browser to tell visits apart. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, those cookies are off by default, so your visits are not told apart or linked together; Google may still receive a cookieless count of pages viewed.

Learn how Google handles this data in Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. To opt out on all websites, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
