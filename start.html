Welcome

Caza is installed.

Three steps and you will see it working on a real listing.

  1. 1

    Pin Caza

    →

    Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Caza so it stays in view.

  2. 2

    Open a Zillow search

    →

    Try one of these, or search anywhere you like.

    Homes for sale in Tampa, FL Homes for sale in Orlando, FL
  3. 3

    Open any listing

    Sales comps

    On the listing, click Sales comps to see nearby sales of the same property type.

What you’ll see

  • A Demand Score badge on each search card, built from saves, views and days on market.
  • Sales comps for the listing, with the closest matches starred.
  • Rental comps from active asking rents.
  • Investor numbers: cap rate, cash flow, DSCR and cash-on-cash, with tax and insurance estimates you can edit.
  • A PDF comps report with your name on it.

Want to run numbers without a listing? Try the free calculators, or read the guides for how each number works.

Caza is independent and not affiliated with Zillow. Figures are estimates, not appraisals.
