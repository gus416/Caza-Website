Free real estate calculators

Four small calculators for the numbers a listing page leaves out. Each one runs in your browser and shows how it got the answer.

For buyers and investors

Property tax after you buy

Your price and state in, an estimated tax bill out, compared with the seller’s. Shows how the state reassesses.

For buyers

Rate drop vs price cut

See the price cut that equals a half-point rate drop on your payment, and the reverse.

For renters

Effective rent calculator

Free weeks, concessions and required fees turned into an effective rent and an all-in monthly cost.

For agents and buyers

Comps price per square foot

Three to six comps in, each price per square foot, the median and an implied value range out.

Results are estimates, not tax, lending or appraisal advice. Nothing you enter leaves your browser. Each tool links to the guide that explains the method: all guides.
