Comps price per square foot

Enter a home and three to six comparable sales to get the median price per square foot and a value range.

Estimates only. Simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter; it is not an appraisal or a broker price opinion. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Price per square foot, quickly

Price per square foot puts sales of different sizes on one scale. Divide each comp’s sale price by its square feet, take the median, and multiply by the subject home’s square feet. The median is steadier than the average when one sale is unusual.

Use comps of the same property type, nearby and sold recently. This tool does the arithmetic only. It does not adjust for condition, lot, age or date, so a rough range is the right way to read it.

Where to use it

The pull-comps guide shows how to find matching sales on Zillow. The low-offers guide uses sales to back a below-asking offer, and the price-cut guide shows how to read a cut against them.

Caza pulls nearby sales of the same property type and shows the median price per square foot on the listing.
