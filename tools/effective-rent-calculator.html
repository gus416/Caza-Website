The advertised rent is not what you pay

“Six weeks free” sounds like a discount on the rent, but it is spread over the lease. And required monthly fees for parking, pets, trash, amenities or utilities push the real cost back up. This calculator shows both effects in one number.

Effective rent is the rent you actually pay across the lease, divided by its months. Add required monthly fees and you get the all-in monthly cost. Compare that figure, not the advertised rent, between apartments.

Is it a good price?

Once you have the all-in number, check it against similar listings. The real monthly cost guide walks through a worked example, Is this rent fair? shows how to compare with nearby listings, and the rental comps guide explains active asking rents and why they are not signed leases.
