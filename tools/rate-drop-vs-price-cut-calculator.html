A lower rate or a lower price?

Both lower the monthly payment, but they work differently. A rate drop reduces the interest charged on the whole loan. A price cut reduces the loan itself. This calculator turns one into the other, so you can see what a half-point drop is worth in price, and what a 3% cut is worth in rate.

Payments come from the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. It covers principal and interest only, so property tax, insurance and mortgage insurance are left out, and your lender’s quote will differ.

Using it in an offer

A seller who will not cut the price may still pay for a rate buydown, and the reverse is also true. Knowing the matching number helps you decide which to ask for.
